## What we collect

| Data | Why | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Name, email, profile photo | Create and show your account | Your Google account (Google Sign-In through Firebase Authentication) |
| Handle, bio, city, interests, cover and profile photos | Your public profile | You |
| Posts, comments, reactions, photos you upload | Show them to other people | You |
| Messages in direct chats and party-room chat | Deliver them to the people you talk to | You |
| Approximate location | Show you in "Nearby" and find people near you. Only while you have "Show me in Nearby" on | Your device, with your permission |
| Microphone audio and camera video | Speak in audio and video party rooms. Never recorded by the app | Your device, with your permission |
| Push notification token | Send notifications | Firebase Cloud Messaging |
| Crash reports and basic device info (model, OS version), your user id | Fix crashes | Firebase Crashlytics |
| Reports and blocks you make | Moderation and safety | You |

## How we use it
To run the app, show your profile and content to others according to your privacy settings, deliver messages and
notifications, keep the service safe (moderation, abuse prevention), and fix bugs.

## Who sees what
- **Other users** see your name, handle, photo, bio, city, interests, posts and rooms. "Private profile" limits posts,
  rooms and events to your followers. "Hide online status" hides when you're online. "Show me in Nearby" controls
  whether people near you can find you.
- **Service providers** that process data for us: Google/Firebase (sign-in, notifications, crash reports),
  LiveKit (real-time audio and video for party rooms), [your hosting and database providers, e.g. AWS, Neon].
- We do not sell your personal data.

## Your choices and rights
- Edit or remove most profile details in **Profile → Edit profile**.
- Turn location sharing off in **Settings → Show me in Nearby** or in your device settings.
- Block or report people from their profile or a room.
- **Delete your account** in **Settings → Account delete karo**. This removes your profile details, posts, follows and
  sessions and signs you out. Conversations you took part in remain for the other person, shown as "Deleted user".
  [Describe how long backups or logs are kept, if applicable.]
